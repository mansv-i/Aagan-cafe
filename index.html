Our story

A courtyard,
a kettle, a corner for you.

Aangan means courtyard, the place where a home gathers. We wanted a café that feels the same: warm, a bit noisy with laughter, and never in a rush.

Every cup of chai is simmered the slow way, with fresh ginger and a pinch of cardamom. Our kitchen makes small batches of comfort food each morning, so what you eat today was made today.
